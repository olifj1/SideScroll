Use + Add Box to build compound collision from several shapes. Drag orange points or edge handles on the selected shape; tap another shape to edit it. Numeric Y positions use the cyan floor as 0. Yellow means a support surface.

CLIMB PATHSInvisible ladder
Path endpointsasset-local metres

The cyan strip is an independent climb controller, not collision. Drag the white centre to move it, the green/blue ends to resize or rotate it, and the pink side handle to change width. A ladder can therefore be Climbable without being Solid.

MECHANISMCounterweight plank

Green = pickup/pivot point. Blue line = free-placement counterweight zone. A log counts when more than 50% of its footprint sits inside the zone.
